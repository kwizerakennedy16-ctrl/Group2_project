Project Name:
NDEJJE CAMPUS CONNECT 


 The Problem Being Solved
Students and staff at Ndejje University often face challenges accessing real-time campus information, ranging from navigating lecture room locations to receiving urgent administrative updates and accessing university resources. Information is currently fragmented across notice boards and various web portals. Ndejje Campus Connect solves this by providing a unified, mobile-first platform that centralizes campus news, academic schedules, and facility locations, ensuring the Ndejje community stays informed and connected through a sustainable digital tool.

Target Users
Students: To access timetables, campus maps, and official university announcements.
Lecturers & Staff: To broadcast department-specific updates and manage course-related communications.
University Administration: To push real-time alerts regarding events, deadlines, or campus-wide emergencies.
